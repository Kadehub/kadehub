Executive Summary

KadeHub is an affordable, all-in-one POS and shop management platform purpose-built for Sri Lankan grocery stores, pharmacies, and retail outlets. It unifies sales, inventory, supplier ordering, customer loyalty, expense tracking, and analytics into a single web application — accessible from any device, with or without an internet connection.

Built on a modern, scalable architecture (NestJS + Next.js), KadeHub runs on a dedicated OVH server with MinIO secure file storage. Every shop gets its own custom subdomain (e.g. yourshop.kadehub.com) and a 14-day free trial before any payment is required.

How It Works — Getting Started

1. Register: Enter your shop name and choose your custom subdomain (e.g. yourshop.kadehub.com). The subdomain can be fully customised during registration to match your brand.
2. Free Trial: All modules are unlocked for a 14-day free trial — no payment required to get started and explore every feature.
3. Choose a Plan: Select Starter, Pro, or Enterprise. Pricing is shown in LKR or USD automatically based on your location.
4. Pay Securely: Pay via OnePay (online card / LANKAQR) or bank transfer. A one-time registration fee of LKR 25,000 applies on first subscription.
5. Go Live: Subscription activates instantly. Your shop is live at your custom subdomain and ready to use.

Infrastructure & Hosting

🖥️

OVH Dedicated Server

Hosted on a high-availability OVH dedicated server — guaranteed resources, no shared neighbours, and full control over the environment for maximum reliability.

🗄️

MinIO Secure File Storage

Product images and shop logos are stored in a private MinIO object storage bucket — secure, scalable, and isolated per tenant with no publicly guessable URLs.

🌐

Custom Subdomain per Shop

Every registered shop gets its own subdomain on kadehub.com (e.g. yourshop.kadehub.com), chosen and customised during the registration process.

Scaling Roadmap

Phase 1 — Now: Modular monolith on shared hosting or cloud. Fast to deploy, easy to maintain.
Phase 2 — Growth: Extract high-traffic modules (POS, Inventory) into standalone microservices.
Phase 3 — Scale: Replace internal event emitter with Kafka. Add Redis caching and rate limiting.
Phase 4 — Enterprise: CDN for product images (CloudFront), PostgreSQL migration, multi-region support.
