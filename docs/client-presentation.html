KadeHub — Point-of-Sale & Shop Management

Affordable, localised, multi-tenant POS and inventory platform for small-to-medium retailers.

Executive Summary

KadeHub is a multi-tenant POS and shop management platform that unifies sales, inventory, supplier orders, customer loyalty, expenses and analytics into a single web app. Designed for quick deployment and low maintenance, KadeHub helps retailers reduce stock loss, simplify cash handling and make faster decisions with built-in reporting.

Key Features

  • Fast POS (cart, receipts, multiple payment methods: CASH, CARD, LANKAQR, CREDIT)
  • Inventory with reorder alerts, batch & expiry tracking
  • Supplier purchase orders & automatic stock receiving
  • Customer CRM with loyalty points and credit management
  • Discounts & promotions (fixed / percentage / date-bound)
  • Shift management and audit logs
  • Offline-first POS queue with automatic sync
  • Built-in analytics: revenue, top products, stock, customer summary

Problems Solved

  • Manual cash reconciliation and missing shift reports
  • Stockouts and overstocks caused by poor reorder visibility
  • Lost sales due to offline conditions or missing credit tracking
  • Time spent on manual purchase orders and supplier follow-ups
  • Lack of simple, actionable reporting for day-to-day decisions

Vision

Empower local retailers to run data-driven, profitable stores by removing operational friction and unlocking digital commerce capability.

Mission

Deliver an affordable, secure, locally-tailored POS and shop management solution that reduces admin overhead, prevents stock loss, and helps retailers grow revenue through insights and retention tools.

Benefits & Value

  • Simplify daily operations — sales to supplier orders in one place
  • Improve cash control with shift-based reconciliation
  • Reduce stockouts and waste via batch/expiry and reorder alerts
  • Increase customer retention with loyalty points and promotions
  • Quick deployment on shared hosting or cloud with low onboarding overhead

Ideal Customers

  • Small grocery and convenience stores
  • Pharmacies requiring batch & expiry tracking
  • Single-location retailers ready for subscription-based upgrades
  • Growing businesses planning multiple outlets

Deployment & Pricing (summary)

Starter: Core POS + Inventory — Pro: Suppliers + Analytics — Enterprise: Multi-store + Integrations. Onboarding, training, and support packages available.
