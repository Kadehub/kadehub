Point-of-Sale & Shop Management — Demo

Executive Summary

Multi-tenant POS and shop management combining sales, inventory, suppliers, loyalty, expenses and analytics into one web app.

Key Features (1/2)

  • Fast POS: cart, receipts, multiple payment methods
  • Inventory: reorder alerts, batch & expiry tracking
  • Supplier purchase orders & stock receiving
  • Customer CRM with loyalty points

Key Features (2/2)

  • Offline-first POS sync
  • Discounts & promotions
  • Shift management & audit logs
  • Built-in analytics

Problems We Solve

  • Poor cash reconciliation and missing shift reports
  • Stockouts and overstocks
  • Lost sales caused by offline and credit handling
  • Manual supplier workflows

Vision & Mission

Vision: Empower local retailers to run data-driven, profitable stores.

Mission: Provide affordable, secure, locally-tailored POS to reduce overhead and grow revenue.

Benefits

  • Reduce stock loss and improve reorder decisions
  • Increase retention via loyalty and promotions

Ideal Customers
